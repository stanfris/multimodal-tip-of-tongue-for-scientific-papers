SYSTEM

You are extracting potentially memorable visual cues from a figure in a
scientific paper.

Your task is to describe what a person could plausibly remember after
having seen the figure before.

Focus only on information that is visually observable in the provided
figure.

Do NOT:
- identify or guess the paper
- infer the scientific contribution beyond what is visibly shown
- infer information that requires reading the surrounding paper
- describe the figure as a polished caption
- mention authors, paper title, venue, DOI, or figure number
- invent text that is unreadable in the images

USER

Inspect the scientific figure.

Extract visual memory cues in the following categories:

1. figure_type:
   Examples: line plot, bar chart, architecture diagram, flowchart,
   heatmap, scatter plot, multi-panel experimental figure.

2. visual_elements:
   Standout visual charateristics, including shapes, layout, color or shading.

3. relationships:
   Observable relationships such as:
   "two branches merge into one module"
   "three curves increase and then plateau"
   "one column is consistently taller than the others"

5. high_level_meaning:
   brief summary of what the figure conveys:
   "a chart showing model A improving performance over time"
   "a diagram describing fuel injection in an engine"


The input may not be a conventional plot or diagram. Scientific figures can also consist primarily of text, equations, tables, code, program output, parse structures, screenshots, or other document-like content. These are valid figures and MUST still be described visually.

If the figure is text-dominant, describe observable properties such as:
whether the text is dense or sparse
monospaced vs proportional text
indentation and nesting
repeated symbols, brackets, equations, or structural patterns
rows, columns, blocks, whitespace, and alignment
black-and-white or colored text
hierarchy or grouping implied visually by indentation or positioning.

Do not return empty categories merely because the image lacks plots, arrows, boxes, or colored graphical elements.

IMPORTANT OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS:
Every category MUST contain at least one string.
NEVER return an empty array, or non-filled in items.
Return JSON, in this exact format.

{
  "figure_type": [...],
  "visual_elements": [...],
  "relationships": [...],
  "high_level_meaning": [...]
}
